# 💼 Business

Business operations and customer relationship management for Symphony enterprise.

## Purpose
Complete business operations hub for Symphony as a business:
- Customer relationship management (CRM)
- Sales operations and pipeline management
- Marketing and lead generation
- Business analytics and intelligence
- Partnership management

## Business Model Integration
Symphony uses Symphony to manage Symphony - the ultimate meta-implementation:
- **CRM**: Symphony agents manage customer relationships
- **Sales**: Symphony orchestration handles sales processes
- **Marketing**: Symphony automation drives marketing campaigns  
- **Analytics**: Symphony intelligence provides business insights

## Universal CLI Integration
```bash
# Customer management
./tools/symphony business customers

# Sales operations
./tools/symphony business sales  

# Business analytics
./tools/symphony business analytics
```

## Key Metrics & KPIs

## Integration Points
- **Organizations**: Customer data flows from `organizations/customers/`
- **Data Analytics**: Feeds business data into `data/analytics/`
- **Operations**: Integrates with `ops/` for business continuity
- **Core Platform**: Uses `core/` agents for business process automation
