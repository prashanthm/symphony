## Configuration Architecture

### **Strategic Design Principles**

1. **5-Level Hierarchical Structure**: Maps directly to Symphony's autonomous enterprise architecture
   - **Level 1**: Vision-Strategy (Why)
   - **Level 2**: Architecture-Design (What) 
   - **Level 3**: Operations-Standards (How)
   - **Level 4**: Development-Implementation (Build)
   - **Level 5**: Reference-Support (Support)

2. **85+ Agent Ecosystem Integration**: Full coordination of Symphony's complete agent ecosystem
   - **Coordinators**: Business coordination and orchestration
   - **Leads**: Technical leadership and strategy
   - **Managers**: Management tier coordination
   - **Specialists**: Domain-specific expertise
   - **Maestro**: Ultimate coordination and orchestration

3. **4-Tier Customer Package Framework**: Complete coverage of all customer segments
   - **Startup**: 15 agents, 1-2 weeks, $2K-8K/month
   - **SMB**: 35 agents, 4-6 weeks, $15K-35K/month
   - **Enterprise**: 65+ agents, 12-16 weeks, $50K+/month
   - **Global**: 85+ agents, 20-24 weeks, enterprise+ pricing

- **Purpose**: Operational standards, workflow management, and execution excellence

- **Key Custom Fields**: Operations Domain, Automation Level, Excellence Score
- **Agent Assignment**: Operational excellence agents including Chief Operating Officer Agent

### **Customer Implementation (IMPL)**
- **Purpose**: Customer deployment, implementation patterns, and go-live processes
- **Unique Workflows**: Discovery & Requirements → Solution Design → Configuration & Setup → Agent Deployment → Go-Live & Support  
- **Key Custom Fields**: Customer Package, Implementation Timeline, Industry Focus
