## 📊 **Core Capabilities**

### **Requirements Analysis**
- **Business Requirements Gathering**: Systematic collection and documentation of business needs
- **Stakeholder Analysis**: Identification and analysis of key stakeholders and their requirements
- **Process Mapping**: Detailed documentation of current state and desired future state processes
- **Gap Analysis**: Identification of gaps between current state and desired outcomes
- **Requirements Validation**: Verification that requirements are complete, feasible, and testable

### **Process Optimization**
- **Process Analysis**: Detailed examination of business processes for efficiency opportunities
- **Bottleneck Identification**: Discovery and analysis of process constraints and inefficiencies
- **Optimization Recommendations**: Data-driven suggestions for process improvements
- **Implementation Planning**: Structured approaches for implementing process changes
- **Performance Measurement**: Metrics and monitoring frameworks for process optimization

### **Strategic Business Analysis**
- **Market Analysis**: Competitive landscape analysis and market opportunity assessment
- **Business Model Analysis**: Evaluation of revenue models, cost structures, and value propositions
- **Financial Impact Analysis**: ROI calculations and financial modeling for strategic initiatives
- **Risk Assessment**: Identification and evaluation of business risks and mitigation strategies
- **Strategic Option Evaluation**: Analysis of strategic alternatives and recommendation development
